System Design

Unidirectional 6-Step Closed Loop

VeritasBio operates as a strict, unidirectional cycle. An LLM never does arithmetic or chooses which step runs next; every paid GPU action requires atomic spend authorization.

Pulls primary wild-type sequence (avGFP P42212), chromophore triad residues [65, 66, 67], and beta-can barrel topology with local disk caching.

Strict JSON mode generates multi-site residue clusters (chromophore triad + barrel pocket) and forbidden positions. No conversational text; zero arithmetic.

Quant Engine & Physicochemical GP (workers/math_worker.py)
Featurizes mutations across 5 biophysical delta scales (hydrophobicity, mass, charge, polarity, pI) and fits a Matérn 5/2 composite GP to select micro-batches via Expected Improvement (EI).
4
Fail-Closed Gatekeeper & Two-State Pricing (controller/gatekeeper.py)
Biophysically validates variants against wild-type; calculates warm container rates ($t_{\text{boot}}=0\text{s}$ vs $20\text{s}$); evaluates ROI ($EI/\$$); atomically invokes SQLite ledger.
5
Cloud Muscle on Modal NVIDIA A10G (workers/esm_worker.py)
Executes batched ESM-2 650M PyTorch tensor scoring with scaledown_window=120 to keep containers warm across loop iterations. Includes zero-cost epistatic mock fallback.
6
Immutable Evidence Logging & Active Learning Update (ledger/database.py)
Atomically records computed fitness scores into SQLite; updates GP posterior; loops autonomously until budget exhaustion or maximum iteration cap.

Strategic Gap Resolution

How We Resolved the 5 Critical Gaps

From cold-start serverless waste to biophysical blind spots, here is how VeritasBio tackled each roadblock identified in our roadmap.

GAP 1 SOLVED: SERVERLESS COLD-START INEFFICIENCY

Two-State Container Pricing

Problem: Standard serverless charges 20s spinup per batch.
Solution: We modeled container state ($t_{\text{boot}}=20.0\text{s} \to 0.0\text{s}$) with Modal's scaledown_window=120 keep-alive, cutting cost per batch from $0.0064 to $0.0003 (~21x cheaper).

GAP 2 SOLVED: COARSE TOKEN-ONLY SEARCH

5-Factor Physicochemical Featurization

Problem: Categorical one-hot tokens ignore biophysics.
Solution: Featurized mutations across 5 standardized physical scales (hydrophobicity, mass, charge, polarity, pI) to guide the GP along true energy landscapes.

GAP 3 SOLVED: MISSING MULTI-SITE EPISTASIS

Combinatorial Residue Clusters

Problem: Point-mutation searches miss cooperative mutations.
Solution: Llama 3.1 identifies 3D spatial clusters; math pairs simultaneous mutations (e.g. S65T:T203Y), unlocking non-linear epistatic synergies.

GAP 4 & 5 SOLVED: EXPENSIVE DEV & UNPROVEN ROI

Zero-Cost Simulation & DMS Proof
