A deterministic cloud economics engine: Unidirectional 6-step loop integrating UniProt RAG, Llama 3.1 JSON priors, Gaussian Process Bayesian Optimization ($EI$), and a fail-closed SQLite ledger. Zero agentic bypass.

Strategic Focus

Where We Must Focus: The 4 Bottlenecks

These are the engineering decisions that make this a breakthrough platform rather than a simple hackathon demo.

BOTTLENECK 1

Math vs. Amino Acids (Quant Featurization)

The Problem: Statistical models understand numbers, but proteins are made of letters (like S65T:Y66H).
Our Fix: Featurize mutations using real biophysical properties: change in charge, water-affinity, and molecular volume.

The Challenge: Standard Gaussian Processes fail on high-dimensional sparse one-hot strings ($O(N^3)$ kernel scaling).
Solution: Dense physicochemical delta featurization ($\Delta\text{Charge}, \Delta\text{Volume}, \Delta\text{Hydrophobicity}$) using AAindex scales.

BOTTLENECK 2

The Single-Mutation Trap (Epistasis)

The Problem: Testing 1 mutation at a time is so cheap that brute force works easily without AI.
Our Fix: Tackle multi-point mutations (combinations of 2, 3, or 4 changes) where there are over 10 billion options.

The Challenge: ESM can score all 4,522 single GFP mutations in 1 pass for ~$0.50. An iterative loop for single mutations is redundant.
Solution: Focus on combinatorial epistasis ($\sim 10^7$ double, $\sim 10^{10}$ triple mutants).

BOTTLENECK 3

The Cloud Cold-Start Penalty

The Problem: Renting a cloud GPU takes 20 seconds to turn on, but only 1 second to do the work. Shutting it off every turn wastes 95% of the money.
Our Fix: Keep the container warm between turns, dropping cost from $0.006 to $0.0003 per batch!

The Challenge: Container cold-boot + PyTorch ESM load is 20s; batch scoring is 1s.
Solution: Modal scaledown_window=120 + Gatekeeper two-state pricing (warm jobs amortize boot penalty).

BOTTLENECK 4

Proving Value (Evaluation Benchmark)

The Problem: Judges will ask: "Did you actually save money, or just build a pretty loop?"
Our Fix: Benchmark against a real lab dataset (Sarkisyan 2016 GFP) and prove we found the brightest proteins for 80% less cost.

The Challenge: Basic Spearman rank doesn't demonstrate cloud dollar savings.
Solution: Cost-to-Top-Decile metric in scripts/evaluate.py: Cost to locate top 5% brightest variants vs brute force.
