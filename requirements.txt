# and Modal call fail and silently fall back to mock planning and mock scoring.
# truststore reads trust anchors from the OS store with verification still ENABLED.
# Opt in at runtime with VERITAS_USE_OS_TRUSTSTORE=1; see controller/tls_trust.py.
